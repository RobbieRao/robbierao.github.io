ContextCam

Bridging Context Awareness with Creative Human–AI Image Co-Creation

A mobile co-creation system turns the situation around a person into ideas for images. Five agents coordinate context selection, topic suggestions, conversation, editing tools and personal preferences.

Xianzhe Fan, Zihan Wu, Chun Yu, Fenggui Rao, Weinan Shi*, and Teng Tu.

CHI 2024 · Full paper

Designing the path from an idea to an image

I organized the Android UI around three decisions: which direction to pursue, which context to use, and whether to generate or revise an image. The floating conversation keeps those decisions beside the person’s current mobile activity.

Three interaction decisions

  1. Choose before generating

    I placed three topic suggestions before the detailed image proposal. Selection and Paint are separate steps, so the person can review the idea before requesting an image.

  2. Keep the inputs visible

    Five context icons stay at the top. Their states distinguish topic-specific relevance from a source the user has turned off; source control remains inside the creation flow.

  3. Name the action and its target

    Paint, Edit and Advice expose different intentions beside free input. Edit acts on the image identified by EditID, while the conversation retains the earlier result and the new instruction.

Across 240 conversations over three days, mean ease of use was 5.88/7. Usage records showed a move from quick recommendations toward self-directed topics, images and selective source control. These are observations of the complete system, not a controlled measurement of individual UI decisions.

Code & materials

I wrote the Android front end published in ArtAgent-android. The repository contains the client and Gradle project; backend_config.json connects it to the separately hosted inference service.
