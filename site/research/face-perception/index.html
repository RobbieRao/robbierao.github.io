Doctoral research · Robbie Fenggui Rao

How does a first impression change when a face moves?一张脸动起来，第一印象会怎样变？

In VR, a participant rates a neutral face. I record EEG as the face smiles, frowns or stays still. Then the participant rates the same face again.

In the lab

1.00×

I selected face shape, skin texture and hair separately for these portraits. Some movements follow a filtered Unity recording. Others interpolate from neutral to an expression fitted from a single photograph. Gaze is simulated for this interactive view.

What moves the rating?

Does someone seem more trustworthy after a smile? A second look alone might change the answer. On matched trials I keep the same face still for the same length of time. That gives me a comparison for changes in perceived trustworthiness, attractiveness and dominance.

I repeat some movements to see how consistently one observer rates the same face. I also align EEG to the start of each expression, so I can examine the response before the observer gives their second rating.

I’ve completed the EEG pilot and am working on the main study.

What does a face rating measure?

In my review, I compared the cues each kind of stimulus lets a researcher vary and the judgments each measurement can capture. Two faces with the same mean rating can still divide observers very differently.

Separating visual cues in a photograph

In DisFace3DNet, I split a face photograph into seven component representations, including shape, skin appearance and lighting. I tested how much prediction accuracy changed when the model had to account for those contributions separately.
