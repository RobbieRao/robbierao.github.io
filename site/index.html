A little more about me

“我致力于寻找世界的bug。”

I’m a designer-researcher who also builds. I take ideas from studio practice into working prototypes, public experiences, research papers, and teachable workflows.

I lead DesignAnything Lab ↗, a creative community working across AI, art, and design. My practice includes workshops, exhibitions, project incubation, and the Lingyuan / Soulscapes AI art exhibition.

I studied Innovative Design at China Academy of Art before joining PolyU’s Asian Ergonomics Design Lab, supervised by Yan Tina Luximon.
