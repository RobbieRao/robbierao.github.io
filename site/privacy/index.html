Website privacy

Understanding visits

This site measures visits to understand which research, CV, and design pages people find useful. The detailed records are private and accessible through the site owner's Cloudflare account.

What is recorded

Private analytics records the page path, time, referring website, public IP address, approximate country and city, network provider, browser and system information, device category, and browser language. URL query parameters and fragments are not recorded. A random identifier stored in this browser helps estimate unique browsers and return visits; a session starts again after a 30-minute gap between recorded page views.

These counts estimate browsers and sessions, rather than identifying individual people. IP-based locations may be inaccurate, and several people may share an IP address.

Your choice

You can turn off private analytics for this browser below. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals also disable it. If browser storage is unavailable, visits may be recorded without a lasting identifier and cannot be reliably deduplicated.

Private analytics preferences require JavaScript.

Storage and access

Records are held in a private Cloudflare D1 database. Raw logs are kept for up to 365 days and may be exported for the site owner's traffic analysis. This site does not expose IP addresses or individual browsing records through a public endpoint. The analytics is not used for advertising or session replay.

The public Homepage views badge uses the separate Hits service. Loading that image contacts Hits; the private analytics preference above does not control that separate page-view counter. GitHub Pages and Cloudflare also process requests to deliver the website and analytics service.
