2022 · Interactive lighting · Physical prototype

LightSpoken

光屿

An interactive lighting and kinetic-installation prototype controlled through a Unity interface.

通过 Unity 界面与 UniOSC 通信控制灯光及蜂巢结构的伸缩运动，将实体制作、灯光编排和机械运动结合为可运行的交互原型。

Physical form and fabrication

The prototype uses a layered, open structure with separate lighting spaces. Cutouts shape the light: small openings reveal point sources, while linear openings work with LED strips. The model combines 5 mm acrylic with mirror paper to sharpen its contours and reflect light between the modules.

Lighting controls

A Unity interface uses UniOSC communication to control the prototype. Single-color, rainbow, and breathing modes offer different light behaviors. Touch controls adjust hue, saturation, brightness, and breathing frequency.

Motor-driven movement

A motor-driven telescopic mechanism raises and lowers the honeycomb modules. The interface provides up, down, stop, and reset controls. Patterned shells make the changing arrangement and the light passing through it visible together.

Programming and testing

The development process connects the control interface, communication code, lighting states, and physical prototype. Mechanical tests informed the clearance between moving modules, while wiring trials addressed cable tangling and unwanted light leakage.

Project team: Fenggui Rao and 俞寒译.
