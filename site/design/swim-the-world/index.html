Swim the World

泳动世界 · 召唤海洋小精灵

An AI co-creation installation connecting drawing, generated characters, and shared public displays, with installations in Hangzhou public spaces.

通过绘画、AI 辅助角色生成与公共屏幕连接公众共创，在杭州亚运相关无废空间及西湖区公共场景落地。

Design & process

The interaction journey moves from scanning a code and choosing a creative theme to drawing a character and sharing it in a collective visual environment.

Public installations & exhibition

  • Hangzhou, 2023. Installed in an Alibaba Cloud zero-waste space associated with the Asian Games and in Xihu District public settings. Project record ↗
  • Selected exhibition, 2023. “AI Connects the Future, Drawing the Asian Games” — Zhejiang university student digital art invitational exhibition. Official selection list ↗

团队：饶冯桂、周致远、臧渠成、庾智滔、崔男、傅蓉。
