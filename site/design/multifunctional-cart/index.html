Multifunctional Cart

多功能购物车

An ergonomic shopping-cart design study developed through posture analysis, structural drawings, 3D modeling, and hands-on prototyping.

从超市购物中的负重、转向与休息需求出发，结合姿态分析、结构草图、三维建模与实体样机，对多功能购物车进行人体工学设计探索。

Design & process

The design process connects shopping needs and posture studies with component development, engineering drawings, and a physical prototype.
