Practice
Digital character · Live demonstration

Display setup

We presented the full-body character inside a rectangular display with an illuminated border. The dark backdrop separated the display from the room, and the tabletop below held the audio equipment. Keeping the physical frame visible gave the figure a clear scale within the setup.

Audio equipment placement

Two compact speakers sat together below the screen, offset to one side. The tabletop gooseneck microphone stood in front of the displayed figure. Its base and the equipment cables remained visible. I read this as one physical arrangement: a figure at full height, an input position in front, and sound equipment below it.

The microphone stem enters the space in front of the display and overlaps part of the character’s lower body in the camera view. This is a concrete placement tradeoff: the input position is visible, but the hardware also occupies some of the image. I check the screen edge, feet, microphone tip, and speaker positions together. The upright display can preserve the whole pose while the lower foreground accommodates the equipment; a tighter crop would lose that relationship. The arrangement establishes where a person can speak without requiring the character to carry an on-screen microphone graphic.

Two recorded poses

The two frames use the same display arrangement and camera view. The character’s arm position changes between them, while the figure remains in the same full-body composition. They provide a direct comparison of pose within a fixed presentation setup.

Character composition

The character combined dark and pale surfaces with cyan accents. We left space around the arms and below the feet so the full pose stayed inside the frame. The wide view included the microphone and speakers as well as the figure; that framing makes the screen and room easier to read together.
