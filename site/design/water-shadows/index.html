2023 · Virtual environment · Projection installation

水陌·渔影

A digital art project connecting a virtual landscape inspired by Hongfan Village, Qiandao Lake, with a fish-shaped projection installation.

以千岛湖洪畈村为背景，通过虚拟场景、粒子影像和鱼骨状投影装置，探索自然景观与数字空间的连接。

My contribution

I developed the outdoor virtual scene, programmed its visual effects, and designed and helped fabricate the physical installation. I also worked on video editing and project writing. The interior exhibition space and architectural remodeling were developed by my teammates.

Real-time technical art

In Unreal Engine, I iterated on Niagara scripts to make fish gather, disperse, blink, and follow spline paths. I also adapted the Ultra Dynamic Sky blueprint and replaced its skybox with imagery made in Stable Diffusion.

For the point-cloud scene, I stored scene-capture depth in the alpha channel and passed it into Niagara to render the landscape as particles.

Structure and material decisions

I designed the installation in Rhino and prepared fabrication drawings. Bent steel tubes produced curves that were too rigid, so we switched to hard aluminum wire inside flexible metal tubing. Sheet-metal skin required too much fabrication work and 3D printing was too costly; projection mesh became the final surface.

Projection and fabrication

I developed the projected particle imagery in TouchDesigner. Geometry COMP instancing created the particles, while Remap TOP sampled the flow field to update their acceleration and velocity. The resulting motion was projected onto the mesh surface.

Project writing and team

I wrote a project study on MR-based immersive cultural-tourism design in Hongfan Village, bringing together the team’s design discussions and implementation work.

Project team: Fenggui Rao, 俞寒译, and 应文康.
